QUESTIONS HOMEOWNERS OFTEN ASK

Is the 15% markup negotiable?
It covers ordering, deliveries, returns, scheduling the plumber and electrician, and replacing damaged items at our cost. If you buy the vanity, top, toilet, faucet or mirror yourself, there is no markup on them (see options).

Why 46 hours of tile work?
82 sq ft of wall tile and 40 sq ft of floor with a niche: about 2.7 sq ft per hour including layout, cuts around the valve and niche, grout and sealing. Tile labour is 18% of the total.

Why a 50% deposit?
Before your start date we buy $6,922 of materials and fixtures and pay $550 in permits; the deposit ($12,330) also holds the crew's 3-week slot. The rest is due on completion.

What if hidden damage is found behind the walls or floor?
We stop, show you, and quote the repair in writing before doing it, at the same rates ($98/h lead installer, $77/h crew) with materials at supplier price plus 15%. Nothing extra happens without your signature; the first 16 sq ft of subfloor is already covered by the allowance.

Why is this more than a basic bathroom refresh?
It is a tub-to-walk-in tiled shower conversion with a moved drain, a sheet waterproofing membrane, a new 20A circuit and a vented fan, not a fixture swap.
