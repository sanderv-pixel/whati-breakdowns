QUESTIONS HOMEOWNERS OFTEN ASK

Do I need a two-stage furnace?
No. A single-stage 96% furnace saves $400 (see options); two-stage runs quieter and evens out temperatures between floors in a two-story home.

Why a new line set?
The manufacturer's R-454B instructions require clean, correctly sized lines. If yours passes our pressure test and is the right size, we flush and reuse it for a $210 credit.

Why a 50% deposit?
The furnace, AC, coil and materials cost $7,095 at distributor price, more than the $5,582 deposit; they are ordered before install day.
